**A slimmer pipeline for frontier models.** Nine agents instead of fifteen, and review dispatches
its seats from your own session, at most four at a time. `plan` writes a short brief by default and
`implement` derives its own breakdown, so there is no separate `tasks` step. The always-on
`AGENTS.md` keeps its rules and drops its tours. Specnaut names model families, never versions.

**Your constitution, read first.** It moves to `docs/constitution.md`, a document your team owns and
no `upgrade` rewrites, and every `/task` entry point and agent opens by reading it.

**`merge: manual` means it.** A landing waits for `merge` in your own prompt; a permission click no
longer releases it. The landing scripts hold themselves, and the command guard reads wrappers,
heredocs, aliases and `cd` targets it used to miss.

**Safer upgrades.** A staging folder reached through a symlink is refused instead of followed, a dry
run reports the moves a real one would refuse, and a link through a regular file no longer aborts
`doctor` or `upgrade`.

On Codex, trust the hooks again with `/hooks` after upgrading. See
[UPGRADING.md](https://github.com/specnaut/specnaut-cli/blob/main/UPGRADING.md).
